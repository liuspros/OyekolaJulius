OYEKOLA JULIUS

Creativity x
Critical Thinking

I've been creative for as long as I can remember. At six years old I was already the best artist in my primary school class — sketching was never a hobby I picked up, it's just who I've always been.

That same restlessness grew into a love for innovation — I wanted to see new things, build things, make things exist that didn't before. Graphic design became the natural outlet, and in 2023 I turned that into something real: Lius Creatives.

Somewhere along the way, I started noticing the same thing in every app I used: this should have that feature. That habit of critical thinking turned into a decision — if I could imagine it, I should be able to build it. So I became a developer too.
