Who I Am

From sketchbooks
to shipped products.

I've been creative for as long as I can remember. At six years old I was already the best artist in my primary school class — sketching was never a hobby I picked up, it's just who I've always been.

That same restlessness grew into a love for innovation — I wanted to see new things, build things, make things exist that didn't before. Graphic design became the natural outlet, and in 2023 I turned that into something real: Lius Creatives. The name is simple — "Lius" from Julius, paired with "Creatives," because that's exactly what it is.

Somewhere along the way, I started noticing the same thing in every app I used: this should have that feature. This could work better. That habit of critical thinking turned into a decision — if I could imagine it, I should be able to build it. So I became a developer too, not to replace the designer in me, but to give it a second set of hands.

I'm a critical thinker and a creative, and above all, I'm passionate about building solutions — whether that's a brand identity or a working product.

Based in Lagos, Nigeria
Originally from Ogbomoso
Studying Computer Science, SQI College of ICT
Building with Next.js · Firebase · Tailwind
