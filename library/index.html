How the library works

01

Open licenses only

Every entry is free to use under a license stated on its card, so you know exactly what you can and can't do with it.

02

Cited, always

Authors are credited, sources are linked, and each card has a citation you can copy in APA or BibTeX.

03

One at a time

Downloads are one file per click, on purpose. There is no download-everything button and a gentle limit per visitor, so the library stays fast and free for everyone.
